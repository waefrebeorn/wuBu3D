## Why this is an ecosystem and not a folder of repos

Every layer feeds the one above it:

* `slermes` runs the agents; `wubuwizard` runs the models; `KAN-WuBu-Memory` gives them a
  memory; `wubucodec` and `wuBu3D` make the media; `wubutv-network-media` is the corpus those
  films and streams are cut from; `WuBuOS` and `WuBuGov` are the platform story underneath.

That is the pitch: **a full stack owned end to end by one person, on consumer hardware, with
every part measured.**
