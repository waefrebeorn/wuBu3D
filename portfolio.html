WuBu3D — a film compiler

Engine · C11 + Python · 2026

A JSON spec compiles to a finished film: geometry, a 21-joint rig with a walk cycle, camera keys, three raster passes, a compositor, a grader, a score and a mux. Two renderers read the same spec — a Cycles path for delivery and my own C11 rasteriser for preview — so a look can be judged in seconds instead of an hour.

Renderer
wuburaster.c, ~330 lines, no dependencies: colour, depth and normal passes, Gouraud shading, height fog, a lantern as a real point light
Rig
wubu3d_rig.py: 21 joints, 1.78 m adult proportions, contact/pass walk cycle, contact sheets verified against the render
Measured
854×480, 144 frames, 2 578 tris/frame: raster 47.5 s, composite 23 s, grade 20 s, mux 5 s — 110 s to a finished MP4
What I cut
Blender's compositor (segfaulted on this build), and a 25-tap denoiser that became a separable 10-tap for 6× the speed at equal PSNR

THE LIGHTKEEPER

Short film · 90 s · 8 shots · 2 160 frames

A keeper walks a plain at night and the light goes out behind him. Procedural geometry, a silhouette-led character, a score written as an A–C–E motif that rises a fourth at the end.

Pipeline
Cycles CPU at 400×225 / 4 spp, 62 min for 2 160 frames, then my own à-trous denoiser, then a NumPy grade (median de-firefly before bloom, S-curve, highlight rolloff)
Delivered
896×504, H.264, −16.9 LUFS master
What I cut
The Blender compositor entirely, after it crashed the render; bloom and halation moved into Python where they can be inspected and iterated in seconds
Lesson
A procedural silhouette reads at 30 m and fails at 3 m — that constraint became the film's staging grammar instead of a defect to hide

A denoiser that fits in a C file

Signal processing · C11 · benchmarked against ground truth

Path-traced frames at low sample counts are noisy. I wrote an edge-aware à-trous filter and measured it against a 64-spp ground truth rather than trusting how it looked.

Result
8 spp noisy 34.53 dB → 35.96 dB at σ=10 (PSNR, 480p); temporal mode lifts a sequence from 25.92 to 35.91 dB
Speed
25 taps 1 604 ms/frame → separable 10 taps 247.9 ms → with temporal reuse 56.6 ms (7.2 Mpix/s)
Honest gap
Intel OIDN reaches 38.11 dB but costs ~17 s/frame on this box; the trade-off is stated rather than hidden
